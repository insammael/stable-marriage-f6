Description:

We prove that a finite poset with at most fifteen elements, width at most
three, and the following common-incomparability property has at most 48
antichains: for every x < y, the elements incomparable with both x and y form
a chain of size at most two. An explicit fifteen-element poset attains the
bound. We show that order-six stable-marriage rotation posets satisfy these
hypotheses by examining convex rotation blocks and their fixed partner pools.
This gives a structural upper bound of 48 stable matchings, matching the lower
bound already provided by Benjamin, Converse, and Krieger in 1995. Equality
forces fifteen rotations, all of size two, and makes all 36 man–woman pairs
attainable. Accompanying Lean files verify the fixed-token, abstract support,
and finite-poset components; the connection to classical rotation semantics
is given in the human proof.

Provenance note: This preprint is accompanied by reproducible verification
artifacts and a partial Lean formalization. The Lean development verifies the
finite-poset core and supporting components; it does not formalize the entire
stable-marriage theorem f(6) = 48 end to end. The source-code and
reproducibility repository is available at
https://github.com/insammael/stable-marriage-f6 and should be linked as a
related resource.
